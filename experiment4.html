B.Tech Course Information

Welcome to the Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) program. This course is designed to provide comprehensive technical education and practical skills required for modern industry challenges.

Course Objectives

  • Understand core engineering fundamentals and concepts.
  • Develop problem-solving and software development skills.
  • Encourage research, innovation, and teamwork.
  • Prepare students for competitive industry roles.

Core Subjects

  1. Data Structures and Algorithms
  2. Database Management Systems (DBMS)
  3. Operating Systems (OS)
  4. Computer Networks
  5. Web Designing & Technology
